Hosting with AirBreakfast

Good stays start with great hosts.

Explore the accommodation styles and guest features represented in our travel-booking demo.

This is a prototype. Host onboarding, property submissions, payments, and booking management are not available. Customer email/password accounts are handled separately by Supabase Auth.

Services in the demo

Guest discovery

Travelers can explore sample stays and narrow results by destination, guest count, dates, price, property type, amenities, and room count.

Property details

Sample listing pages show accommodation details, amenities, nightly prices, and guest capacity.

Reservation requests

The request form sends the entered contact and stay details to Formspree. It does not create or confirm a booking.

Accommodations to explore

Our sample catalog features houses, apartments, villas, and cabins—from city stays to beachfront, poolside, and mountain retreats.

About AirBreakfast

AirBreakfast is a small front-end project exploring how travelers can discover places to stay through clear search, helpful property details, and a straightforward browsing experience.

It is an independent demo and is not affiliated with Airbnb. Listings and host information are illustrative sample content.
