How can we help?

Find answers about searching, filters, saved stays, and reservation requests.

AirBreakfast is a demo. It does not provide live availability or confirmed bookings.

Finding a stay

Search by destination, dates, and guest count. You can also narrow the sample listings by category, price, property type, amenities, bedrooms, and bathrooms.

Date search uses each demo listing’s minimum-stay rule. It does not check a live availability calendar.

Go to search

Saving favorites

Use the heart button on a listing to save or remove a stay. Signed-out favorites are stored in this browser; when you sign in, saved stays sync to your account and are available on other devices.

Reservation requests

The request form checks your dates and guest count, then sends the details you enter—including your email—to Formspree.

Submitting a request is not a reservation or confirmation. Do not include sensitive information.

Accounts and sign-in

Email/password accounts use Supabase Auth after the site owner configures a project. If email confirmation is enabled, follow the confirmation link before signing in. Use “Forgot password?” to request a reset link.

Authentication is available after the site owner configures a Supabase project. Only email/password sign-in is supported; social sign-in is not enabled.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I find a stay for my dates?

The sample catalog filters dates using minimum-stay rules, not live availability. Try a longer stay or remove the dates.

Did I book a place after sending a request?

No. The form sends a request through Formspree, but this demo does not confirm, charge for, or manage reservations.

Can I recover favorites on another device?

Yes, if you were signed in when you saved them. Sign in to the same account on another device to access synced stays. Favorites saved while signed out remain in that browser.
